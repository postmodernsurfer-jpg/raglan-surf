THE WEDGE
NEWPORT BEACH · SURF SPOT GUIDE
LOCAL LORE / NO. 01
KNOW THE BREAK

Two waves. One lineup.

A legendary wedge-shaped wave at the entrance to Newport Harbor, known for its shifting peaks, heavy shorebreak and unpredictable closeouts.
SWELL TO WATCH
SOUTH · SOUTHWEST
Southerly swells can wrap around the jetty and help set up the classic wedge effect.
WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
REFLECTION + REBOUND
Wave energy can reflect off the rock jetty and meet incoming swell at an angle.
WEDGE HISTORY
The harbor jetty helped shape the wave. Over generations, The Wedge became a Southern California icon for bodysurfing, bodyboarding and spectacular wipeouts.
RESPECT THE SHOREBREAK · WATCH FROM A SAFE DISTANCE
